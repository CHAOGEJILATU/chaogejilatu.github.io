Profile

Turning Earth data into decisions.
I study how climate change reshapes agriculture and land systems by combining remote sensing, GIS, and statistical modeling. My work focuses on building reproducible analyses and clear, decision-ready insights from complex geospatial data.

Science, data, and decision support for climate-resilient futures.

Research Interests

  • Climate change and food security
  • Land use / land cover change and environmental sustainability
  • Remote sensing, GIS, and statistical modeling
  • International collaborations in East Asia and global networks
